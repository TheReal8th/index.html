      Squad data

      Paste one player per line as year,team,player,games,career, one row per season. games is games for that club that season; career is the player's career total and is optional. Games are added up per player per club across the four eras (1998–2000, 2001–2009, 2010–2019, 2020–2026). A header row is fine.

      Points and eligibility are based on games — how many games that player played for that club, in that era — not career. A career column is optional and currently just along for the ride. Anyone over 100 games for that club/era stays in the squad, but Lock it in will tell you they're too well known rather than pretend they don't exist.

      Player data from Rugby League Project. Spot an issue, or want something removed? Email Joey.

        How to play

        Classic

        Pick a year, tap Team to draw a random club, then name players who turned out for that club in that era. Only players with 100 games or fewer for that club, in that era count — someone who barely played there still counts even if they were a star elsewhere.

        Points depend on how obscure the player is:

        • 1 game — 100 pts
        • 2–5 games — 50 pts
        • 6–10 games — 20 pts
        • 11–20 games — 10 pts
        • 21–50 games — 5 pts
        • 51–100 games — 1 pt

        No timer. Three hints per round. First to 250 points wins.

        Blitz

        Pick a year and tap Start. Name as many players as you can from any club in that era with under 20 games in that era (added up across clubs if they played for more than one), before the 60 or 90 second timer runs out. Same points as above apply. Once it starts, the timer can't be restarted — only ended by time running out or Give up.

        League Legend

        No year to pick — tap Team for a random club and name anyone who's ever played for them, 1998 to 2026. No timer, and every player counts — even the club's biggest legends, worth whatever their points tier gives (which may be next to nothing for a true great). Give up to see who you missed and log your score.
